How it works

From the group chat to a scoreboard.

Pick the pool, name it, pick its colour, and drop the link in the chat. Friends join with a name, an email and a password, on any phone, with nothing to install.

Pick before kickoff

Each pick locks at its own game's kickoff, so Thursday night locks Thursday and Sunday's games stay open. One tap picks the favourites; the pool's voice says who still has to pick.

Questions people ask

Before you start one.

Is it money?

No. Coins are the pool's score and nothing else: never bought, never cashed out. It is bragging rights and a crown.

Do my friends need an app?

No. The link opens the pool in the browser on any phone, and it can be added to the home screen like an app. Joining is a name, an email and a password.

How does against the spread work?

Switch it on when you start the pool. Each pick takes the market's line at the moment you save it and keeps it, so a line that moves later never changes your pick. The favourite has to win by more than the line, the underdog can lose by less, and landing right on it is a push, worth nothing.

What breaks a tie?

Every week has a tiebreaker: the total points in the week's last game, usually Monday night's. Level on points for the week, the closest call takes it; level at the end, the closest on the last week played wins the pool.

What if someone forgets to pick?

Switch on "Forgot a pick? The favourite" and a game nobody picked takes the market's favourite when it starts, instead of scoring nothing. In last one standing, give everyone two or three lives so one bad Sunday isn't the end.

Can we join mid-season?

Yes. A pool starts from the next week with a game still to come, and each pick locks at its own kickoff, so a late joiner still plays every game left. Last one standing starts fresh from the week you choose.
