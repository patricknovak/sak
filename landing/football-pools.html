How it works

Questions

Before you ask.

Is there any real money in it?

No. Pools are played for points and a crown. Supercoins are never bought, never sold, never cashed out.

Do my friends need an app?

No. They tap your link and join with just a name, in the browser on any phone. Add it to the Home Screen for alerts if you like.

How does against the spread work?

Switch it on when you start the pool. Each pick keeps the line it was made at, so a line that moves later never changes it, and landing right on the number is a push.

What breaks a tie?

Each week has a tiebreaker: the total points in its last game. Level on the week, the closest call takes it.

What if someone forgets to pick?

Switch on the favourite for a missed pick, and a game nobody picked takes the favourite at kickoff. In last one standing, give everyone up to three lives.

What about the playoffs?

They open in January: the bracket, a pick on every playoff game, the Eliminator, prop sheets and squares. Start one the day they open.
