Questions

Before you start one.

Is it money?

No. Supercoins are never bought, sold or cashed out, and there are no prizes of value. The pool remembers who called it, and that is the prize.

We're already a few episodes in. Is it too late?

No. A pool started now gets every question still to close, and everyone starts level, with the drops already gone by.

Will somebody spoil it?

Each question closes as the episode goes on air, and your host settles it once it has aired. Strangers are never in your pool, and the chat stays inside it.

Do my friends need an app?

No. They tap the link, type a name and they are in on that phone. An email later keeps the seat on another.

Who decides the answer?

Your host, by the rule written on the question. Everyone sees the note, and a question with a mistake in it is voided and every Supercoin goes back.

What happens after the finale?

Keep the pool and its people, and ask the next show's questions from the Host page. Two new seasons are expected in the new year.
