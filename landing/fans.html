Inside every zone

A front office's read.

Five pages a club: the overview, the analysis, the outlook, the history and the fans' pulse.

  • The verdictA grade from every measure that matters in the sport, each ranked in the league, with a luck check on what won't last.
  • The outlookThe season and its spring played a few thousand times: the playoff and title chances, the seeds, the first-round opponents.
  • The playersThe leaders, every player's numbers and who is hurt. In the NHL, every goalie against the danger of the shots he faced.
  • The movesIn the NHL, a trade or an injury moves the outlook the same hour, priced in goals a game.
  • The front officeThe bigger questions with a verdict each, the targets that fit the need, and the calls for the coaches.
  • The historyFor the NHL, every season from its record books: leaders, trophies, the draft. For the other leagues, every season since 2002.
The fans' voice

Your say, with just a name.

Your calls

One call each on the season's questions, in the sport's words: who starts in goal, the MVP, the one to move, grades for the GM and the coach. Counted in the totals, never shown one by one.

The board

Ideas, questions, insights, facts with their sources and fixes, voted up or down. When the analysis gets something wrong, a fix is made in the open.

The pulse

Every call's split and the top posts on one page anyone can share, print or download, the club's own front office included.

Questions

Before you jump in.

Is it free?

Yes. Every club's zone is free to read, with nothing to sign up for. Joining in (a call, a post, a vote) takes only a name.

Who sees what I add?

Your posts show your first name and initial, never more. Your calls and votes are only ever counted in the totals, never shown one by one.

Where do the numbers come from?

Only public sources, read fresh: for the NHL its own statistics, its player and puck tracking and its record books; for the NFL, the NBA and the Premier League, public score feeds and the games as they finish. Nothing behind a login and nothing bought.

Is it run by the leagues?

No. The fan zones are made by Super Pools from the leagues' public numbers and are not affiliated with any league or club.

Can we play on it?

Yes. Start a pool for the fans in your chat: one pick a night, the Cup called all season, your own club questions. Supercoins, never money.

How do I help?

Make your calls, post an idea, a question, an insight or a fact, and vote. When the analysis gets something wrong, post a fix: the editors read every one.
