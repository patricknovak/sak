What's inside every zone

A front office's read, for every fan.

The verdict

A grade and a read of the season from every measure that matters in the sport (46 in hockey), each ranked in the league, with a luck check on what won't last.

The outlook

Playoff and title chances from thousands of simulated seasons (the title, Europe and the drop in the Premier League), the pace, the race and the schedule still to come.

The players

The club's leaders, every player's numbers, the ages and who is hurt; in the NHL every goalie measured against the danger of the shots he faced.

The front office

The bigger questions with a verdict each, the moves for the general manager with the league's players who fit, and the calls for the coaches.

The history

For the NHL, every season the franchise has played, its leaders, records, trophies and draft, straight from its record books. For every league, the fans' own knowledge base.

The fans

Calls, ideas, questions, insights and fixes, voted by the fans, and a fan pulse that puts it all on one page.

The fans' voice

What the fans think, on one page.

Each fan makes their calls for the season, in their sport's own words: who should start in goal or at quarterback, the MVP so far, the one player to move, the biggest need, how far this spring or where in the table, and a grade for the general manager and the coach. Ideas and questions rise on the fans' votes, facts build the club's knowledge base, and the fan pulse shows it all to anyone, the club's own front office included.

Open by design

Every number, in the open.

The code that builds every zone is open source under the MIT License, on GitHub, every formula with it. The fans' own data is open too: each club's pulse and every post, from the pulse page or straight from app.superpoolsai.com/api/fans/EDM (any NHL club's three letters, or NHL) and /api/fans/nfl/KC (any other league and club). When the analysis gets something wrong, a fan posts a fix and it is made in the open.

Questions people ask

Before you jump in.

Is it free?

Yes. Every club's zone is free to read, with nothing to sign up for. Joining in (a call, a post, a vote) takes only a name.

Who sees what I add?

Your posts show your first name and initial, never more. Your calls and votes are only ever counted in the totals, never shown one by one.

Where do the numbers come from?

Only public sources, read fresh: for the NHL its own statistics, its player and puck tracking and its record books; for the NFL, the NBA and the Premier League ESPN's public numbers and the games as they finish. Nothing behind a login and nothing bought.

Can the club see what fans think?

Anyone can: each club's fan pulse puts every call, the top ideas and the questions on one page to read, share, print or download, the club's own front office included.

Is it official?

No. The fan zones are made by fans, from the leagues' public numbers, and are not affiliated with any league or club.

How do I help?

Make your calls, post an idea, a question, an insight or a fact, and vote. When the analysis gets something wrong, post a fix: the editors read every one. The code is open source under the MIT License, on GitHub, for anyone who wants to look under the hood or build on it.
