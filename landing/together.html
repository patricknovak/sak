Questions

Before you ask

Is there any real money in it?

No. Pools run on coins that cannot be bought, sold or cashed out, and there are no prizes of value. It is bragging rights, a crown and a pool that remembers who called it.

Do I need to know anything about odds?

No. A price is just the group's chance: 64% means most of your friends think so. Put coins on the answer you believe. If you are right, you are paid more the fewer people agreed with you.

Who decides the answer?

The pool's host. Every question carries its rule from the moment it is asked, and it locks once anyone calls it. The host settles it with a note everyone sees; match results settle themselves.

Do my friends need an app?

No. They tap the link you send, make an account with an email and a password, and they are in.

Is this connected to Netflix?

No. Super Pools is not affiliated with Netflix or Love Is Blind. A show pool is fans making calls about what airs, the way the group chat already does.
