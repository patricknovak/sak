Questions

Before you start one.

Is it money?

No. Supercoins are never bought, sold or cashed out, and there are no prizes of value. The pool remembers who called it, and that is the prize.

Will somebody spoil it?

Questions are about what airs this week, they close as the episode drops, and your host settles them the same night. Strangers are never in your pool, and nothing on it tells you who wins.

Who decides the answer?

Your host, by the rule written on the question when it was asked. Everyone sees the note, and a question with a mistake in it is voided and every Supercoin goes back.

Do my friends need an app?

No. They tap the link, type a name and they are in on that phone. An email later keeps the seat on another.

Do I need to understand the numbers?

No. The number is the group's chance, and a right call collects more the fewer people agreed with you.

What happens when the show ends?

Keep the pool and its people, and ask the next show's questions from the Host page. The table and the chat carry over.
