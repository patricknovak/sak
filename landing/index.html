Questions

Before you ask

Can we bring our league?

Yes, for hockey fantasy. Roster import from Fantrax, ESPN and Yahoo is live today (paste a link or open Yahoo). Past seasons can be imported or pasted as a final table. A full one-tap move of settings and every past season is still being finished; until then we also set leagues up with you. Keepers, the draft room, lineups, trades, side challenges and the league voice are ready on day one. Fantasy football and soccer fantasy are on the 2027 road.

Is there any real money in it?

No. Side challenges and calls run on Supercoins: never bought, never sold. If your league plays for dues, that stays between friends, the way it always has; Super Pools can track who has paid, nothing more.

How do the prices on a question work?

Each answer's price is the group's chance of it, and the prices add up to 100%. Calling an answer pushes its price up; a share of the right answer scores one Supercoin, so calling it early, before the group agrees, pays the most. You can take them off before a question closes.

What does the league voice cost us?

Nothing yet. Every league and pool gets the full voice free while paid tiers are still being built. The plan is a weekly recap with Plus and the full voice (daily recaps, rankings, grades and answers in the chat) with Premium. It only ever posts what your league asks it to.

Does everyone need to download an app?

No. They tap the link, make an account with an email and a password, and they are in. Add it to the home screen and turn on alerts if you like.

Which sports can we play?

Sports pools run today on the NHL and NBA seasons game by game, the NFL, MLB, the Stanley Cup and NBA playoffs, the Premier League, MLS, the WSL and NWSL, and March Madness, each with a sport centre for scores, standings, news and injuries (NHL centre is the deepest). Fantasy drafts are hockey only for now; soccer and basketball fantasy follow on the same engine in 2027. Questions pools cover anything else you ask.

What happens on game night?

Everyone in the league shares one live room: the scoreboard moves the moment the box score does, every swing shows up as it lands with the room's reactions, and when your player scores you hear about it wherever you are in the app. On draft night the clock keeps time to the second.
