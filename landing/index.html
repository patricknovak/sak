Questions

Before you ask

Can we bring our league?

Yes. A league on Yahoo comes over with its teams, rosters and every past season Yahoo kept; any other site's final tables can be pasted in. Keepers, the draft room, lineups, trades, side bets and the league voice are ready on day one.

Is there any real money in it?

No. Side bets and calls run on coins that cannot be bought, sold or cashed out. If your league plays for dues, that stays between friends, the way it always has; Super Pools can track who has paid, nothing more.

How do the odds on a question work?

Each answer's price is the group's chance of it, and the prices add up to 100%. Calling an answer pushes its price up; a share of the right answer pays one coin, so calling it early, before the group agrees, pays the most. You can sell back before a question closes.

What does the league voice cost us?

The weekly recap comes with Plus; the full voice (daily recaps, rankings, grades and answers in the chat) comes with Premium. It only ever posts what your league asks it to.

Does everyone need to download an app?

No. They tap the link, make an account with an email and a password, and they are in. Add it to the home screen and turn on alerts if you like.

When does soccer arrive?

Matchweek pools on the Premier League and MLS come first, this season: every match home, draw or away, closing at kick-off and settling itself at full time. Soccer fantasy follows on the same engine.
