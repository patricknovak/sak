Questions

Before you ask

Is there any real money in it?

No. Pools run on coins that cannot be bought, sold, transferred or cashed out, and there are no prizes of value. It is bragging rights, a crown and a pool that remembers who called it. Cash dues in a sports league stay what they always were among friends: bookkeeping between you.

How do the prices work?

Each answer's price is the pool's chance of it, and the prices always add up to 100%. When you put coins on an answer, its price rises; when friends back another answer, it falls. A share of the right answer pays one coin, so calling it early, before the pool agrees with you, pays the most. You can sell back before a question closes.

Who decides the answer?

The pool's host. Every question carries its rule from the moment it is asked, and it locks once anyone calls it. The host settles it with a note the pool sees, can void it with every stake refunded, and every coin that moves is a line in the ledger.

Do my friends need to download an app?

No. They tap the link you send, make an account with an email and a password, and they are in. They can add it to their home screen and turn on alerts if they like.

Is this connected to Netflix?

No. Super Pools is not affiliated with Netflix or Love Is Blind. A show pool is fans making calls about what airs, the way a group chat already does.

Can we bring our hockey league?

Yes. Leagues on Yahoo come over with their teams, rosters and every past season Yahoo kept; any other site's final tables can be pasted in. Keepers, the draft room, lineups, trades, side bets and the league voice are ready on day one.

When does soccer arrive?

Soccer pools (gameweek calls, a score predictor and last one standing on the Premier League and MLS) come first, this season. Full soccer fantasy follows on the same engine.
