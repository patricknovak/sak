Questions

Before you ask

Can we bring our league?

Yes. From Yahoo we bring over every past season Yahoo kept, and final tables from any other site can be pasted in. For now we set up your league with you, teams and rosters included; moving leagues over from the big fantasy sites on their own is being built now. Keepers, the draft room, lineups, trades, side bets and the league voice are ready on day one.

Is there any real money in it?

No. Side bets and calls run on coins that cannot be bought, sold or cashed out. If your league plays for dues, that stays between friends, the way it always has; Super Pools can track who has paid, nothing more.

How do the odds on a question work?

Each answer's price is the group's chance of it, and the prices add up to 100%. Calling an answer pushes its price up; a share of the right answer pays one coin, so calling it early, before the group agrees, pays the most. You can sell back before a question closes.

What does the league voice cost us?

Nothing yet. Every league and pool gets the full voice free while paid tiers are still being built. The plan is a weekly recap with Plus and the full voice (daily recaps, rankings, grades and answers in the chat) with Premium. It only ever posts what your league asks it to.

Does everyone need to download an app?

No. They tap the link, make an account with an email and a password, and they are in. Add it to the home screen and turn on alerts if you like.

Which sports can we play?

Pools run today on the NHL, the NFL, MLB, the NBA playoffs, the Premier League, MLS and March Madness, each with its own centre for scores, standings, news and injuries. Fantasy leagues start with hockey; soccer fantasy follows on the same engine.

What happens on game night?

Everyone in the league shares one live room: the scoreboard moves the moment the box score does, every swing shows up as it lands with the room's reactions, and when your player scores you hear about it wherever you are in the app. On draft night the clock keeps time to the second.
