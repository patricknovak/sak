Three things we say

The chores, done every night.

01

It runs itself.

Points land from the box scores as the games happen, and every player locks at his own puck drop. The scoreboard asks the NHL for every clock and score every 20 seconds while a game is on.

Every finished game is re-checked for three days, and every Monday for the last three weeks. Set lineups sixty days ahead, or let the AI-optimized auto-pilot cover the nights you forget.

02

It talks back.

Every league names its own voice. The founding league's is Garry: an AI-enhanced morning column after every night with games, Monday rankings, the afternoon lineup nudge before puck drop and answers in the chat.

Before a scheduled post goes out, its numbers, @tags and table gaps are checked against the league's table, and a column that fails falls back to the plain table. It is a language model and can be wrong, as the Terms say.

03

Built by a league.

The SaK Superleague has played for keepers since 2013. Its fourteenth season, the first on Super Pools, has run on it every night since the keeper deadline.

Keepers, a draft room with a TV board, trade review windows and IR rules come baked in. The founding league feels every bug first.

Questions

Before you ask.

Is there any real money in it?

No. Calls, side bets and the Book run on Supercoins: never bought, never sold, never cashed out. If your league plays for dues, that stays between friends; Super Pools can track who has paid, nothing more.

Does everyone need to download an app?

No. Friends tap your link and join a pool with just a name, right in the browser. A fantasy league asks for an email and a password. Add it to the Home Screen for alerts if you like.

Who pays?

Nobody, today. When billing ships, a pool pays once a season, never per person: the host pays or splits it, and members never see a bill.

Can we bring our fantasy league?

Yes, for hockey. Rosters come over from Fantrax, ESPN and Yahoo, past seasons from all three, and scoring from Fantrax. The one-tap move of every setting is still being built, so for now we set your league up with you. Ask for your league.

Which sports can we play?

Pools run today on the NFL, the NHL, the NBA and soccer, scored from public score feeds. The Premier League and the WSL run every matchweek to May. MLS and the NWSL are at the end of their seasons: a pool started now plays one round (MLS Decision Day on 7 November, the NWSL quarter-finals on 6 to 8 November), and later playoff rounds open as they're set. The NFL playoffs open in January, the college basketball tournament in March, and the Stanley Cup and NBA playoffs in April. Fantasy leagues are hockey only for now.

What about the shows we watch?

Start a questions pool. Each question closes as the episode airs or drops, everyone calls it in Supercoins, and a host you know settles it by the rule written on the question.

What does the league voice do?

Your league names it; the founding league's is Garry. It writes the morning column, the Monday rankings and the afternoon lineup nudge before puck drop, answers questions in the chat and talks trash on request. It has a daily budget, and the commissioner tells it what the league is about.
