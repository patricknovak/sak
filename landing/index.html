The Supercoin

One fun currency, never money

Every account has a Supercoin wallet. Every pool grants its GMs an allowance for the season and tops it up for the draft, the deadline and the playoffs. Head-to-heads, props, pools and the Book settle in Supercoins from the box scores, and a site-wide prediction market takes your word on the Cup, the scoring title and the trade of the year.

Supercoins cannot be bought, sold or cashed out, which keeps them fun and keeps us out of the betting business. Cash dues stay what they have always been in a pool among friends: bookkeeping, with the balances on the record. No payments are handled.

Every coin that moves is a line in the ledger, visible to the pool, so nobody ever wonders where the money went.

Questions

Before you ask

Does the assistant run my league?

No. You do. The commissioner sets the rules, rules on disputes and decides what the voice is allowed to say. Super Pools does the bookkeeping: scoring, locks, settlement, lineups, the record.

Can we bring our Yahoo league?

Yes. The Yahoo import brings over your teams and rosters so the first season starts from where you are, keepers included.

Is it only hockey?

Today, yes: the NHL, built by a hockey pool for hockey pools. Soccer and basketball come next season on the same engine, then cricket (free to play), baseball, football and pools for golf and F1. A sport appears on the site only when it is live and its data is licensed.

Can I run a free pool?

Yes, a real one: one sport, points scoring from presets, a draft with a clock, daily lineups, standings, chat, polls and history, with no ads. The paid tiers add custom scoring, keepers, imports, the voice and the rest, priced per pool and never per GM.

Is there real money in it?

No. The side bets and the Book use Supercoins, which cannot be bought or cashed out and are worth bragging rights. Cash stays what it has always been in a pool among friends: bookkeeping, with the balances on the record. No payments are handled.
