Sixteen questions, every call on the page

The answers marked settled here are made up to show how a pool reads. They are not the real Season 11 results.

A tick means the question settled that player's way. A cross means it did not. Questions still open show the call and the coins behind it, with no tick or cross yet.

Everyone starts with 1,000 Goblets and gets 250 more on each drop night, so by this point each player has had 1,500 to work with. Worth above that comes from calls that paid out and from open calls still worth more than they cost.

A player can take coins back off a question at any time before it closes, which frees them to call again elsewhere. That is why the coins across a row add up to more than the 1,500 a player has been paid so far.

Scoring

How the scoring works

Every answer has a price, and the price is the pool's chance it happens. A price of 25% means the group thinks one in four. A call buys shares at that price, and a right answer pays one Goblet a share, so a call few people agreed with pays more than an obvious one. Prices move as friends call the other way, and a player can take coins back off a question any time before it closes. When the last question settles, the player worth the most takes The Golden Goblet. Goblets are play coins: never bought, never worth anything outside the pool, and the score is all they measure.

Questions people ask

Before you start one.

Is this a real pool?

No. It is an illustration built to show the shape of a Love Is Blind pool: the questions, the calls and the Table. The names are made up, the settled answers are made up for the example, and nothing on this page is read from a live pool.

Who decides the right answer?

The pool's host. Every question is written with a rule for settling it, the host settles it from the episodes by that rule, and everyone in the pool sees a note when it settles. A host can also void a question and refund the coins if something on air makes it unclear.

Do my friends need an app?

No. A pool opens in the browser on any phone and can be added to the home screen like an app. Joining takes a name, an email and a password.
