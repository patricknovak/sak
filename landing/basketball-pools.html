How it works

Three steps to tip-off.

01

Start it, send one link

Pick the pool, name it and drop the link in the group chat. Friends join with just a name, in the browser.

02

Pick before tip-off

Each pick locks at its own tip-off, so a late joiner still plays every game left. A reminder goes out before each lock.

03

It settles itself

Results land as each game goes final, overtime included. The table moves, and a climb rings the phone.

Questions

Before you ask.

Is there any real money in it?

No. Pools are played for points and a crown. Supercoins are never bought, never sold, never cashed out.

Do my friends need an app?

No. They tap your link and join with just a name, in the browser on any phone. Add it to the Home Screen for alerts if you like.

Which pool should we play?

For a group that watches every night, the daily streak. For the office, weekly pick'em or last one standing.

Can we start one now?

Yes. A pool plays from the next game still to come, and each pick locks at its own tip-off, so nobody is late for the games left.

What about win totals?

They lock at opening night, so they closed on 20 October for this season. They open again before the next one.

When do playoff pools open?

In mid-April: the bracket, pick the series, prop sheets, squares by quarter and the sweepstake.
