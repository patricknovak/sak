Questions

Before you ask.

Is it free, and is there any money in it?

It is free, and there is no money in it. Pools are played for points and a crown. Supercoins are never bought, never sold and never turned into money. If your office collects a few dollars a head, that stays between you.

When can I start an NFL playoff pool?

Today. The 14-team field is set on Sunday 10 January 2027 and the bracket opens that night. A pool started earlier plays the NFL as it stands, and its host adds the bracket in one tap when it opens, so the crew is already in when it matters.

When does the bracket lock?

At the first wild card kickoff, Saturday 16 January 2027 at 4:30 pm Eastern. Every other playoff pick locks at its own kickoff.

How is the bracket scored?

A point for each wild card winner, two in the divisional round, four for a conference champion and eight for the Super Bowl. Flat scoring, a point a game, is the other choice. Ties go to the closest call on the Super Bowl's total points.

What if someone joins after wild card weekend?

They can't fill in a bracket, but a pool can run more than one game: pick every game, the Eliminator and the prop sheets all take a late joiner from the next round.

Do my friends need an app?

No. They tap your link and join with just a name, in the browser on any phone. Add it to the Home Screen for alerts if you like.

Can we run Super Bowl squares in the same pool?

Yes. Add the grid from the pool's Host page, under Add a game, once the field is set. It shares the pool's members and chat. How the squares work.
