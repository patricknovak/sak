How it works

Three steps to kickoff.

01

Start it, send one link

Pick 10 by 10 or 5 by 5, what a square costs in Supercoins and how many each person can hold. Drop the link in the chat; people join with just a name.

02

The grid fills, the digits are drawn

Everyone claims squares, or lets the grid pick for them. The digits 0 to 9 are drawn for each team when the grid is full or at kickoff, from a recorded seed anyone can check.

03

Every quarter pays itself

The last digit of each score names the square after the 1st quarter, at the half, after the 3rd and on the final. The chat hears who won before the commercial ends.

Questions

Before you ask.

How do Super Bowl squares work?

A grid has one team across the top and the other down the side. Everyone claims squares, then the digits 0 to 9 are drawn for each side. At the end of each quarter, the last digit of each team's score names the winning square: at 17-13, the square where 7 meets 3.

Is it free, and is there any money in it?

It is free, and there is no money in it. Squares are claimed with Supercoins, which everyone in the pool is given and which are never bought, sold or turned into money. If your group collects a few dollars a square, that stays between you.

When are the numbers drawn?

The moment the grid is full, or at kickoff if it isn't. Nobody knows their digits when they claim, and the draw comes from a recorded seed anyone can check afterwards.

What if the grid does not fill?

It plays as it is. When the score names an empty square, its Supercoins pass to the next claimed square along the row. For a small group the 5 by 5, 25 squares with two digits a side, fills fast.

How does it pay?

After the 1st quarter, at the half, after the 3rd quarter and on the final score: 20, 20, 20 and 40 per cent of the Supercoins paid in. Or choose the final score alone. Overtime counts toward the final.

When can I set it up?

The Super Bowl grid opens the night the playoff field is set, Sunday 10 January 2027, before the teams are known. Before that, a grid goes on any NFL game of the week, which is a good way to try it with the same crew.

Do my friends need an app?

No. They tap your link and join with just a name, in the browser on any phone. Add it to the Home Screen for alerts if you like.
