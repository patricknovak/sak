Questions people ask

Before you start one.

Is it money?

No. Coins are the pool's score and nothing else: never bought, never cashed out. It is bragging rights and a crown.

Do my friends need an app?

No. The link opens the pool in the browser on any phone, and it can be added to the home screen like an app. Joining is a name, an email and a password.

Which pool should we play?

For a group that watches every night, the daily streak: one pick a night, the longest run of right picks wins. For the office, weekly pick'em or last one standing. For the hockey crowd who wants players, the box pool: one player from each box, no draft night. For the serious ones, a fantasy league.

Can we move our fantasy league over?

Yes. Paste your Fantrax or ESPN league link (or open your Yahoo league) and every team's roster comes back matched to ours, with your history. Points or categories, season total or head-to-head, keepers, best ball or the guillotine.

Is there an NBA one?

Yes. The NBA season runs the same way from opening night, October 20: weekly pick'em, last one standing and the daily streak, one pick a night.
