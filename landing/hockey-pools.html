How it works

Pick Classic, ten boxes of six, or Quick, five of five, and drop the link in the group chat. Poolies join with just a name.

02

Questions

Before you ask.

Is there any money in it?

No. Entries play for points and a crown, and Supercoins are never bought, never sold, never cashed out. If your pool collects dues, that stays between friends.

How does the box pool work?

The top players are dealt into evenly matched boxes: forwards, then defence, then goalies. Each poolie takes one from every box. A goal or an assist is a point, a goalie win two, a shutout one, and the host can change them before the deadline.

When is the deadline?

The first puck drop of the pool's first night. Every entry locks then, and the table counts live for a week, four weeks or the rest of the season.

Can two poolies take the same player?

Yes. Boxes are not a draft, so the same player can be on every entry, and any number of poolies can play.

Is there a playoff pool?

In April: the playoff box pool before round 1, dealt on expected playoff games, plus the bracket, pick the series, prop sheets, squares by period and the sweepstake.

We run a keeper league. Is that here too?

Yes, on the fantasy side: keepers, the draft room with a clock, live points at every puck drop and a league voice. Bring your league.
