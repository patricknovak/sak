The league voice

It talks back.

Your league names its voice; the founding league's is Garry. It writes an AI-enhanced column after every night with games, report cards on keepers, the draft and trades, and answers in the chat.

Its lineup calls are AI-optimized: one swap and the points it gains. Before a scheduled post goes out, its numbers, @tags and table gaps are checked against the league's table, and a column that fails falls back to the plain table. It is a language model and can be wrong, as the Terms say.

Proof

Built by a league.

The SaK Superleague has played for keepers since 2013. Its fourteenth season, the first on Super Pools, has run on it every night since the keeper deadline.

The scoreboard asks the NHL for every score and clock every 20 seconds while a game is on. All 1,344 regular-season games are scored as they go final, then re-checked for three days, and every Monday for the last three weeks.

Questions

Before you move it.

Is there any real money in it?

No. Side bets and the Book run on Supercoins: never bought, never sold, never cashed out. If your league plays for dues, that stays between friends; Super Pools can track who has paid, nothing more.

Can we keep our league's history?

Yes. Past seasons come over from Yahoo, ESPN and Fantrax, one link a season, and they stay readable on every plan, whether or not the league ever pays.

Can I start a league myself tonight?

Not on your own yet. Ask for your league and we set it up with you: rosters, scoring, keepers and past seasons. The one-tap move of every setting is still being built.

Do my GMs need to download anything?

No. It runs in the browser on any phone; each GM joins from their seat's invite link with an email and a password.

What does it cost?

Nothing today, and nothing is charged until billing ships. The plan: a league of up to 12 teams on presets stays free for good; custom scoring, keepers and the full voice would be a season fee for the whole league, never per GM. See the planned prices.
