Questions

Before billing ships.

Is there any real money in it?

No. Supercoins are never bought, never sold and never turned into money, and no plan sells coins or anything coins can win. If your league plays for dues, that stays between friends; Super Pools can track who has paid, nothing more.

Is the free plan a trial?

No. The plan is that Free stays free for good: every kind of pool for a group of up to 30, a fantasy league of up to 12 teams on presets, chat, Supercoins and imports, with no ads. A member never pays.

Do I have to pay for everyone?

No. When billing ships, the plan is a fee per pool, so one payment covers the whole league. We plan to let anyone chip in a share, and members never see a bill.

The big sites are free. Why would we pay?

The big free sites are paid for with ads and their own rules, and one now sells “fewer ads” as a perk, up to US$80 a person a year. Our free pool has no ads, and Premium is planned at CA$99 for the whole league, CA$8.25 a team.

What happens to our pool when billing ships?

Nothing is charged today. When billing ships, the plan is that a pool that starts in 2026-27 keeps every feature free until its season ends, then pays the founding rate, 30% under list, while it renews without a gap, and founding pools hear the final list prices by 1 July 2027.

Will the price change mid-season?

No. When billing ships, the plan is that a season is paid once, at the price shown, and a founding pool keeps the founding rate while it renews.

Does it renew by itself?

No. When billing ships, the plan is that the host pays each season on purpose, and a pool that doesn't play doesn't pay. Ask within 30 days of paying and the season would be refunded in full.

What if you shut down?

Your history is readable on every plan, every day, not only on the day a service closes. It stays readable whether or not the league ever paid.

We already pay dues. Isn't this a rake?

No. The planned fee is a flat season fee for the software, like booking the room. Nothing your league does among yourselves passes through us: no rake, no entry fee to us, no house, Supercoins never bought or sold, and the ledger shows every coin's reason.

Is the league voice a gimmick?

It writes from the box scores and the league's own table, and it trash-talks on request. Before a scheduled post goes out, its numbers, @tags and table gaps are checked against the league's table, and a column that fails falls back to the plain table. It is a language model and can be wrong, as the Terms say. It keeps to a daily budget, and the commissioner names it and tells it what the league is about.
