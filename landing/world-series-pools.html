Questions people ask

Before you start one.

Is it money?

No. Supercoins are the pool's score and nothing else: never bought, never sold. It is bragging rights and a crown.

Do my friends need an app?

No. The link opens the pool in the browser on any phone, and it can be added to the home screen like an app. Joining is a name, an email and a password.

When do picks lock?

The series pick locks at the first pitch of Game 1. A prop sheet, a squares grid and the day's pick each lock at the first pitch of their own game, so a friend who joins on Sunday still plays every game left.

How does scoring work for the series pick?

Points for the right winner and more for the right length. Classic scoring gives 8 for the champion and 3 more for the right number of games; flat and all-or-nothing scoring are a tap away when you start the pool.

How do baseball squares work?

Friends claim squares on a grid with Supercoins. The digits are drawn at the first pitch, and the last digit of each side's runs names the winning square after the innings you choose and at the final.

What if the matchup isn't set yet?

Start the pool now. The series pick opens the moment both pennant winners are known, and anyone who hasn't called it gets a nudge in the hours before Game 1.
