Questions

Before you ask.

Is there any real money in it?

No. Pools are played for points and a crown. Supercoins are never bought, never sold, never turned into money.

How does Call the score work?

Three points for the exact score, one for the right result, and the match you bank counts double. Each call locks at its own kick-off.

Which competitions are on?

The Premier League and the WSL every matchweek to May. MLS and the NWSL are at the end of their seasons: a pool started today plays one round, MLS Decision Day on 7 November or the NWSL quarter-finals on 6 to 8 November, and later playoff rounds open as they're set. The Champions League isn't on yet.

What happens with a draw?

In pick'em a draw is a pick of its own. In last one standing a draw or a loss puts you out, and a called-off match lets you through.

Do my friends need an app?

No. They tap your link and join with just a name, in the browser on any phone. Add it to the Home Screen for alerts if you like.

Can we start mid-season?

Yes. A pool plays from the next matchweek still to come, and each call locks at its own kick-off.
