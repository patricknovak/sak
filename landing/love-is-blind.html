Questions

Before you ask.

Is it money?

No. Goblets are never bought, sold or turned into money, and there are no prizes of value. They are the score and nothing else.

Is there a Season 12 pool yet?

Not yet. Season 12 is expected in February on the show's pattern. The Season 12 pool opens when Netflix announces the season. Start a questions pool any time.

Will somebody spoil it?

Questions are about what airs, each closes as its drop lands, and your host settles it. Strangers are never in your pool, and the chat stays inside it.

What if someone joins late?

They start with the same Goblets as everyone else, drops already gone by included, so a friend who joins at episode 6 can still win it.

Can the host write their own questions?

Yes. Once the couples are known, your host adds questions about them, each with the rule for settling it.

Is this connected to Netflix?

No. Super Pools is not affiliated with Netflix or Love Is Blind. A show pool is friends making calls about what airs, the way the group chat already does.

Until then, ask your own.

The next show, the office bake-off, the wedding. One link for the chat, and friends join with a name.
