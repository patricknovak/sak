How it works

Three taps from the group chat to a scoreboard.

01

Start a pool, send one link

Name it, pick its colour, and drop the link in the chat. Friends join with a name, email and password, then they're in — on any phone, with nothing to install. The link already asks the first question.

02

Call it, watch it move

Everyone starts with 1,000 Goblets and gets 250 more on every drop night. Put them on the answer you believe and the price moves with every friend who agrees or doesn't. Take them off before it closes if you change your mind.

03

Settle it, crown someone

Questions close at 3 am Eastern as each drop lands. The host settles them from the episodes, right calls score Goblets, and the table moves. Whoever is worth the most at the reunion takes The Golden Goblet.

Questions people ask

Before you start one.

Is it money?

No. Goblets are the pool's score and nothing else: never bought, never sold. It is bragging rights and a crown.

Do my friends need an app?

No. The link opens the pool in the browser on any phone, and it can be added to the home screen like an app. Joining is a name, an email and a password, and the first call is placed before the form is even done.

What if someone joins late?

They start with the same Goblets everyone else was given, including the drops that already happened, so a friend who joins at episode 6 can still win it.

Spoilers?

The chat is inside your pool and nobody outside it sees a word. Questions are about what the show airs, so nothing in the pool tells you anything the episodes haven't.

Can the host write their own questions?

Yes. Once the couples are known, the host adds questions about them, with the rule for settling each one. They close when the host says.
